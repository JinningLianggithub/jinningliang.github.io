Simulating AGN Feedback

Introduction

Active Galactic Nuclei (AGN) exert powerful influence on their host galaxies. These feedback processes regulate star formation, impact gas inflows, and shape the co-evolution of galaxies and supermassive black holes (SMBHs).

Most large-volume cosmological simulations treat AGN feedback using simplified prescriptions with constant coupling efficiency — the fraction of AGN luminosity converted to kinetic energy. However, observations and small-scale accretion-disc models (especially UV line-driven wind models) suggest that the coupling efficiency should depend strongly on the black hole accretion rate, typically following a power law.

In this project, I introduce a new AGN feedback model where the coupling efficiency varies with the Eddington ratio, motivated by UV line-driven wind theory, and study its impact on disc galaxy evolution.

Simulation Framework

Our simulations use the SWIFT hydrodynamic code with the COLIBRE galaxy formation model. We construct idealised Milky Way-like galaxies including:

  • A static dark matter halo
  • Stellar disc
  • Cold gas disc
  • Hot circumgalactic medium (CGM)
  • A central SMBH

Key components of the modelling:

  • Thermal AGN feedback, representing shock-heated disc winds
  • Variable coupling efficiency:
    \[ \eta = \min(N_\eta \, \dot{m}^{\alpha_\eta}, 1) \] where:
    • \(\dot{m}\) = Eddington ratio
    • \(N_\eta, \alpha_\eta\) are free parameters from UV line-driven wind theory
  • BH growth regulated by Bondi accretion with turbulence/vorticity corrections
  • Self-consistent star formation, cooling, SN feedback, and chemical enrichment

Feedback Model: Variable Coupling Efficiency

UV line-driven wind models predict that the kinetic luminosity of AGN winds scales with the Eddington ratio as:

\[ L_{\text{kin}} / L_{\text{bol}} \propto \dot{m}^{2.6} \]

This motivates replacing the traditional constant efficiency with a physically motivated model that evolves with accretion state.

We explore:

  • Fiducial variable model: \(\eta \propto \dot{m}^{2.6}\)
  • Constant-efficiency control model: \(\eta = 0.05\)
  • Variations in slope and normalization: \(\alpha_\eta = 0.5, 1.5, 3.0\), \(N_\eta = 10, 300, 3000\)

These tests reveal how sensitive AGN wind feedback is to the underlying physics of SMBH accretion.

Results

1. Black Hole Growth Changes Dramatically

Lower coupling efficiency → weaker AGN feedback → much faster SMBH growth. This is the most important outcome.

  • High-\(\eta\): AGN self-regulates quickly → low accretion
  • Low-\(\eta\): sustained high Eddington ratios → rapid BH growth

2. Outflow Strength Depends Strongly on Efficiency

  • High-\(\eta\): short, powerful bursts; strong hot bubbles
  • Low-\(\eta\): weaker but long-lasting outflows
